# E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis

### Turning Sales Data Into Actionable Business Intelligence

An end-to-end business intelligence project analyzing e-commerce sales performance, profitability, discount strategies, and product-level performance using Tableau.

## 📊 Interactive Dashboard

**[View the Live Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/views/E-CommerceSalesProfitabilityAnalysis_17947533155400/Dashboard1)**

## 📈 Executive Summary

This project examines e-commerce sales data to understand revenue performance, identify profitability opportunities, and evaluate how discounting and product categories influence business results.

The objective is to move beyond reporting sales figures and uncover insights that can support smarter pricing, inventory, and business decisions.

### Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | $2.30M |
| Total Profit | $286.40K |
| Profit Margin | 12.47% |
| Total Orders | 5,009 |

## 🎯 Business Objectives

This analysis investigates five important business questions:

1. Which product sub-categories generate the most and least profit?
2. How do discounts influence overall profitability?
3. How do sales and profit fluctuate over time?
4. Is revenue growth consistently translating into profit?
5. Where should the business focus to improve profitability?

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills

- **Tableau:** Interactive dashboards, data visualization, and KPI reporting
- **Data Analysis:** Revenue, profit, margin, and discount evaluation
- **Business Intelligence:** Performance monitoring and decision support
- **Data Storytelling:** Translating metrics into business questions and actionable insights

## 🔎 Analysis Overview

### 1. Sales & Profit Performance

Analyzes monthly sales and profit trends to evaluate business performance over time.

### 2. Discount Impact on Profit

Explores the relationship between discount levels and profitability to identify potential pricing risks.

### 3. Product Sub-Category Profitability

Compares profitability across product sub-categories to identify stronger and weaker performers.

### 4. Executive KPI Monitoring

Summarizes overall business performance using four key metrics: sales, profit, profit margin, and order count.

## 💡 Preliminary Business Insights

The dashboard establishes an overall profit margin of approximately 12.47% on $2.30 million in sales.

The discount and sub-category visualizations provide opportunities to investigate whether certain pricing practices or product groups are reducing profitability.

Specific recommendations should be based on the observed chart results and validated against the underlying data.

## 🚀 Potential Business Recommendations

- Review low-performing product sub-categories for pricing or cost issues.
- Evaluate discount strategies against their impact on profit.
- Monitor sales and profit together rather than relying on revenue alone.
- Investigate monthly performance changes to support forecasting and planning.

## 📌 Project Outcome

Developed an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to support data-driven business decisions through KPI tracking, profitability analysis, and clear visual storytelling.
